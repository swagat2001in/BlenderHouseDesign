Concept design · Puri, Odisha

Puri Haveli Home-Hotel

A home-cum-guesthouse on a 30' × 40' plot with the road on the north. The ground floor is open stilt parking; the first floor has three guest rooms with attached baths and a small kitchenette. The columns, stair and roof slab are set up for two more floors. The same plan is shown in three styles.

Jaipur-pink lime plaster with cream outlines, jharokha balconies, stone chhajja eaves, cusped arches, jaali screens, a crenellated parapet and domed chhatris.

Laterite walls with khondalite stone bands, corbelled stone openings instead of arches, temple-style stone balusters, bay windows and roof pavilions with stepped pidha roofs, a rekha tower over the stair, a Konark-wheel relief at the entrance and a Pattachitra mural in the parking.

Egg-white lime plaster with red-oxide bands, granite pillars with teak bracket capitals in the parking, peacock-green louvred shutters under stained-glass transoms, carved Burma-teak balconies under Mangalore-tile canopies, a carved teak entrance door with brass studs, a teak colonnade at the rear sit-outs, a stucco crest on the parapet, and a tiled terrace pavilion with an oonjal swing. Athangudi-tile floors inside.

Ready for more floors

  • 12" × 12" columns on one grid, with footings sized for G + 3.
  • Capped starter bars stick out 2 ft above the roof at every column.
  • The roof slab is designed as a floor slab, so it becomes the 2nd-floor floor as-is.
  • The dog-leg stair keeps going up. The mumty on the roof is a light enclosure to remove later.
  • Bath and kitchen plumbing sits in vertical stacks that repeat on each floor.
  • The reception for the 2nd floor opens off the stair landing, the same way the first-floor lobby does.

Check before building

  • The jharokhas and roof chhajja project about 1'-9" past the north plot line, over the road edge. Make them shallower if the line has to be kept clear.
  • Bath 1 has no outside wall. Give it an exhaust fan with a duct to the facade. Fit a chimney or exhaust fan over the kitchenette hob.
  • Side windows face the neighbours. Keep them high or screened with jaali.
  • Puri is coastal and in a cyclone zone. Ask a structural engineer for the design, and use corrosion-resistant rebar cover and good roof waterproofing.
  • This is a concept model for discussion, not a construction drawing.
